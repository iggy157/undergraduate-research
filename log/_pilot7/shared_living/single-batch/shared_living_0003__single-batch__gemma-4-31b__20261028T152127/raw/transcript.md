## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Rin**: Setting up every morning takes way too long, and those unplanned shuffles are a total nightmare. We can't have a chaotic system.
- **Toshio**: We are paying for empty space, and every single move costs us thousands of yen. Constant changes are financially irresponsible.
- **Mizuki**: Some people arrive much later than others, and the current plan leaves too many people without windows. It's not equitable.
- **Shion**: The noise levels vary wildly across the room, and it takes days to get a chair and monitor set up correctly.
- **Yumi**: Sitting together actually helps us work faster and helps new people learn the ropes just by listening.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Rin**: If it takes nine minutes just to plug in, a free-for-all sounds exhausting.
- **Toshio**: Exactly. If we move people around frequently, the facilities quotes will drain our budget.
- **Mizuki**: If we go with a first-come, first-served system, the early birds always get the best spots. That's not fair.
- **Shion**: And if we move constantly, I'll never get my workstation adjusted to a comfortable setting.
- **Yumi**: But if we stay in fixed blocks, we might miss out on those spontaneous helpful conversations.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Rin**: Maybe rotating? But that sounds like more of those "fortnight of interruptions" shuffles.
- **Toshio**: Rotating blocks would be a recurring expense for power and network. It's inefficient.
- **Mizuki**: Fixed desks seem the most stable, but we have to decide who gets the window seats.
- **Shion**: Fixed desks are the only way to ensure I'm not stuck in a high-decibel zone with a bad chair.
- **Yumi**: I agree that fixed desks help, as long as we group people who actually need to talk.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Rin**: I just don't want to waste my gaps between meetings. Fixed seems safest.
- **Toshio**: I concur. Fixed desks minimize costs and administrative waste.
- **Mizuki**: As long as we assign them logically and not just by habit, I'm for it.
- **Shion**: It's the only logical choice for physical and mental comfort.
- **Yumi**: If we group by function, we keep the synergy. I'm in.
