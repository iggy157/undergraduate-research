## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Rin**: The bin house at the station block smells really bad in summer! And the supervised mornings take almost an hour.
- **Midori**: Shelters are expensive, over two hundred thousand yen, though there is a subsidy. We also spend thirty-four thousand yearly on maintenance.
- **Shunichi**: Most complaints come from the person whose gate the bins are by. Also, the station block fought over repairs and cleaning.
- **May**: The truck comes quite early, between 7:40 and 8:15. Those supervised mornings are about fifty minutes each.
- **Asuka**: Not all our frontages are equal; one is very narrow and another has a driveway. Also, filing for a build takes four months.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Rin**: If it smells that bad, we shouldn't build a box! That would be a disaster for the summer.
- **Midori**: I agree. And the cost of building one seems quite high for some of us to handle.
- **Shunichi**: It seems the current location is the main source of friction. We need to address that specifically.
- **May**: Fifty minutes every few weeks isn't too bad, but that early truck time might be a problem for some.
- **Asuka**: Moving it frequently would require constant notices to the ward, which is a bureaucratic headache.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Rin**: But if we just leave it there, the person at the gate stays sad!
- **Midori**: True, but we must find a solution that doesn't break our budgets or our friendships.
- **Shunichi**: If we rotate it, we might just move the argument around the block instead of solving it.
- **May**: I just don't want one person doing all the work. A schedule would be fairer.
- **Asuka**: A rotation is only fair if the spaces are equal, which they aren't. Some spots are too narrow.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Rin**: I don't like the idea of standing there in the morning. It sounds boring.
- **Midori**: Maybe we can find a middle ground that isn't too expensive or too demanding.
- **Shunichi**: We have to weigh the smell and the cost against the fairness of the location.
- **May**: Let's see what the actual options are before we decide.
- **Asuka**: Agreed. Let's look at the formal proposals.
## Round 3
