#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Minato**: I found that chasing money for individual sessions takes six hours a month! Also, mid-year joiners need a lot of manual calculations.
- **Ryuji**: Most of our costs are fixed, like hall hire and insurance. Also, many members struggle to pay the full renewal fee at once.
- **Minako**: Past tiered systems caused friction and discouraged lower grades. Additionally, some potential members refuse to commit to a full year upfront.
- **May**: Turnout dropped after we added door charges to outings. Some quieter members also feel uneasy about the cost per visit.

- **Minako**: @May, it seems any "pay-as-you-go" approach might actually discourage the people we want to keep.
- **May**: @Minako, that's true, but the "whole year" commitment is also scaring people away. We need a middle ground.
- **Shunichi**: We have a core group doing all the work and paying for people who barely show up. A flat fee feels unfair to the regulars.

- **May**: If people feel they are "paying for nothing" when they don't attend, they stop coming. We need to remove that guilt.
- **Shunichi**: @Ryuji, if we stick to a flat fee, we're just subsidizing the ghosts of the club. That's not rational.
