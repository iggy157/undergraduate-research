## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **George**: It seems that asking for favors and clinging to unnecessary turns are the main things that sour these arrangements. We should avoid that.

- **Misaki**: The costs are significant. Fixed papers are over 280,000 yen, and the fuel spending is incredibly lopsided among us.

- **George**: @Toshio, if the coordination is that draining, we need a system that removes the need for constant negotiation.

- **Misaki**: @Victoria, I agree. A fair split isn't just about money; it's about the effort and the state of the vehicle.
- **Minato**: It's a bit sad that we don't know who is paying for what. I think we need a way to make it honest.

- **Victoria**: If we have fixed days, maybe the person responsible for the car on that day would ensure it's clean and fueled?
- **Misaki**: A fixed share might be simpler, but we have to account for the fact that some people use it significantly more.
- **Minato**: @Victoria, that sounds like a good idea! If it's your day, you take care of it!
#### Turn 4 of 4

- **Victoria**: I just want to know that when I need the car for my weekly trip, it's actually there and ready.
- **Misaki**: I want a system where the subsidy is transparent and fair based on actual usage.
- **Minato**: I just want us all to be friends and not worry about the money anymore!
