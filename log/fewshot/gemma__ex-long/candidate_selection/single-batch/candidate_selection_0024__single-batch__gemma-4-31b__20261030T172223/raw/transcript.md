## Round 3
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Minato**: The candidates are Woodland Walk, Market Plaza, Sports Terraces, and Wetland Restoration. I think the Wetland one might be risky with the clay.
- **May**: Market Plaza is hard-surfaced, so it's cheaper to maintain. But the habitat fund won't pay for it!
- **Shizue**: Woodland Walk reminds me of the dead trees on the avenue. I don't trust it.
- **Victoria**: Sports Terraces could use those youth league volunteers. That would take the pressure off our seven staff members.
- **Shunichi**: Wetland Restoration solves the drainage and heat issues, but it might fight the people walking to the tram.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Minato**: @Shunichi, if the Wetland plan uses boardwalks, maybe people can still get to the tram?
- **May**: @Victoria, but who pays for the courts? The habitat fund won't cover the Sports Terraces' surfacing.
- **Shizue**: @Minato, the overflow basin worked well. Maybe the Wetland plan is the most durable option?
- **Victoria**: @May, the city budget must cover the rest. But the volunteer support for sports is a huge operational win.
- **Shunichi**: @Victoria, sports courts don't help with the August heat or the drainage callouts. We need a functional landscape.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Minato**: I like the idea of the Wetland, but I'm still scared of the slippage.
- **May**: I'm leaning toward Market Plaza. It's the most affordable to keep up long-term, even if the initial funding is tricky.
- **Shizue**: I agree with the Wetland Restoration. It's like the basin; it works with the land rather than against it.
- **Victoria**: I still think Sports Terraces is the most realistic for our current staffing levels.
- **Shunichi**: Wetland Restoration is the only one that addresses the actual environmental failures of the site.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Minato**: I'll go with the Wetland. It sounds the most interesting and helpful for the city.
- **May**: I can't ignore the upkeep costs. I vote for Market Plaza.
- **Shizue**: For durability and peace, I vote for Wetland Restoration.
- **Victoria**: For the sake of the staff and volunteers, I vote for Sports Terraces.
- **Shunichi**: Logic dictates we fix the drainage and heat. I vote for Wetland Restoration.
