## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Ryuji**: My findings suggest a risk of instability. Supplier windows for certain tech are unreliable, and winter road closures can isolate parts of our network.
- **Yumi**: It's sad, but some outer wards lost their bus links. However, the old outreach stops were actually more popular than the main building midweek!
- **George**: The funding structure is problematic. Grants only cover session fees for a year, and last year's vehicle running costs were significantly over budget.
- **May**: History repeats itself. A digitisation project from six years ago failed to index half its images, but partner-run evening groups are still thriving.
- **Daisuke**: We have retired tradespeople who can help, but we have no one on the payroll trained to supervise outside facilitators. Also, our tool certification expert retired.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Ryuji**: @George, if vehicle costs are overrunning, any proposal relying on a fleet might be a liability, especially with the road closures I found.
- **Yumi**: @Ryuji, but the people in those wards are stranded! We have to find a way to reach them, even if the roads are tricky.
- **George**: @Yumi, I understand the need, but we cannot commit to a service that drains our operating budget after the first year of grant funding.
- **May**: @George, that's why we should look at the partner-run model. My data shows those groups survived without constant internal funding.
- **Daisuke**: @May, that sounds nice, but who will supervise them? I found that we lack the internal training to manage outside facilitators.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Ryuji**: @Daisuke, that is a critical point. If we lack the skills to supervise, we are relying on conditions we cannot control.
- **Yumi**: @Daisuke, maybe the community can help? If the neighbourhood associations are already doing it, can't we just support them?
- **George**: @Yumi, support still requires a budget. If the grant expires and the operating budget is thin, the service will collapse.
- **May**: @George, the evidence from six years ago shows that community-led groups are the only ones that actually last.
- **Daisuke**: I just don't want the current staff to be overwhelmed by things they aren't trained for.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Ryuji**: We need a solution that is low-maintenance and doesn't depend on volatile supply chains or weather.
- **Yumi**: But it must be accessible. We can't ignore the people who lost their bus links.
- **George**: It must be financially sustainable beyond the initial grant.
- **May**: And it should be based on what has actually worked in this system before.
- **Daisuke**: It needs to fit the skills we actually have on the roster.
