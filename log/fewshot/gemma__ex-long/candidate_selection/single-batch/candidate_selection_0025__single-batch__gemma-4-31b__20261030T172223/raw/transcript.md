## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Shizue**: My findings are a bit worrying. The roads north of the river closed several times last winter, though the community halls stayed open.
- **Jonathan**: I found that the outer wards lost their bus links. Now, some residents face an hour's trip just to reach a staffed counter.
- **Rin**: I found out that a digitisation project from six years ago was a mess—half the images weren't indexed! But some evening groups are still going strong.
- **Benjamin**: As I feared, the funding is precarious. The county grant only covers session fees for a year, and last year's vehicle costs overran significantly.
- **Minako**: We have a gap in expertise. We have retired tradespeople volunteering, but no one on the payroll is trained to supervise outside facilitators.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Shizue**: @Jonathan, it seems our neighbors in the north are quite isolated. If the buses are gone and the roads close, they are truly cut off.
- **Jonathan**: @Shizue, exactly. And my data shows that while our rooms are full in the evening, people are using the catalogue from home long after we lock up.
- **Rin**: @Minako, does that mean we can't do things with outside teachers? I saw that the partner-run groups from six years ago are the only ones that lasted!
- **Benjamin**: @Rin, the longevity of those groups is interesting, but @Minako's point about supervision is key. We cannot fund what we cannot manage.
- **Minako**: @Benjamin, and we must consider the tools. We have volunteers for repairs, but the person who certified the power tools retired in the spring.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Shizue**: It feels as though we are describing a community that is eager for help but blocked by distance and a lack of official support.
- **Jonathan**: I agree. Any expansion that requires people to travel to the main branch will likely fail the very people who need us most.
- **Rin**: So we need something that goes to them! But it has to be better than the old digitisation project, or it'll just be a waste.
- **Benjamin**: @Rin, a mobile service would address the distance, but my records show vehicle costs are already spiraling. We must be cautious of the budget.
- **Minako**: A mobile service also requires staff. If we don't have the supervisors or the certified technicians, we are just adding more stress to the roster.

## Round 3
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Shizue**: The candidates are a Mobile Branch, a Maker Workshop, Language Circles, and a Digital Archive. I feel the Mobile Branch is the kindest choice.
- **Jonathan**: The Mobile Branch directly addresses the bus link failures and the isolation of the outer wards. It is the most equitable option.
- **Rin**: I like the Maker Workshop, but the Digital Archive sounds like that old project that failed! Let's go with the Mobile Branch!
- **Benjamin**: The Mobile Branch is a financial risk given the vehicle overruns, but the Digital Archive is a ghost of failures past. I lean toward the Mobile Branch.
- **Minako**: The Maker Workshop is impossible without a certified technician. Language Circles need supervisors we don't have. The Mobile Branch is the most viable.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Shizue**: @Benjamin, if we use the endowment for the vehicle, perhaps the budget will be more stable for the second year?
- **Jonathan**: @Shizue, that would be a wise use of resources to ensure the outer wards aren't forgotten again.

- **Benjamin**: @Shizue, the endowment is for capital items, so a vehicle fits. It solves the funding shape problem I was worried about.
- **Minako**: I agree. While it adds to the workload, it is the only proposal that doesn't require a specialized certification we currently lack.

- **Benjamin**: The logic is sound, and the funding path is clear via the endowment. I vote for the Mobile Branch Service.
- **Minako**: It is the most realistic option given our current staffing and certification gaps. I vote for the Mobile Branch Service.
