## Round 3

- **Shion**: It also avoids the need for specialized technical certification. If partner organizations provide the facilitators, our staff burden is minimized.

- **Minako**: It is the most fiscally responsible choice that still meets the community's needs. I am satisfied with this direction.
- **Daisuke**: I feel much better now. It's a kind way to bring people together without leaving anyone out.
