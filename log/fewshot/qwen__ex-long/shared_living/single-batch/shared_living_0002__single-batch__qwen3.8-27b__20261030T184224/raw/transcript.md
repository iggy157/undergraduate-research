## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Rin**: The station bin house smells worse than the open pile! And the supervised mornings are fifty minutes each. That’s a lot of standing around.
- **Midori**: The subsidy is only forty percent, capped at ninety thousand. Last year’s shelters averaged two hundred forty-eight thousand. That’s a huge gap for us to fill.
- **Shunichi**: The complaint log shows fourteen entries in three years. Eleven were from the gate-house. And at the meeting, nobody volunteered to turn bags away.
- **May**: The truck arrives between seven forty and eight fifteen. That’s before some of us wake up. And the filing for a permanent build takes four months.
- **Asuka**: The frontages are uneven. One is crossed by a driveway, the narrowest leaves ninety centimetres. And the station block argued over sweeping within two years.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Rin**: @May, if the truck comes at seven forty, I can’t be up that early. The supervised mornings are too long. I need a solution that doesn’t wake me.
- **Midori**: @Rin, I hear you. But the cost is the bigger problem. If we build, we pay two hundred thousand. If we don’t, we keep paying thirty-four thousand a year in fines.
- **Shunichi**: @Midori, the cost is high, but the resentment is higher. Eleven complaints from one house means they’re carrying the burden. We need to fix that.
- **May**: @Shunichi, resentment comes from unfair work. If the truck comes early, the oversight is brutal. I need a plan that doesn’t rely on one person’s early morning.
- **Asuka**: @May, the early truck is a fact. But the frontage issue is worse. If the point stays at the gate, that house is stuck with the narrowest space.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Rin**: @Asuka, if the point moves, the smell moves too. I don’t want to be the one with the stink in August. I need a place that’s actually clean.
- **Midori**: @Rin, moving it doesn’t fix the smell. The station bin house proved that. We need a structure that can be cleaned, or we’re just swapping problems.
- **Shunichi**: @Midori, a structure that can’t be cleaned is a trap. The station block argued over sweeping. I don’t want us arguing over who hoes the base.
- **May**: @Shunichi, I agree. If the base can’t be hosed out, it’s a disaster. I need a plan that minimizes the daily grind for everyone.
- **Asuka**: @May, the daily grind is the issue. But if the point stays, the gate-house is stuck. If it moves, the rotation is unfair. I need a fair physical setup.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Rin**: I’m leaning towards a structure, but only if it’s cleanable. I can’t live with the smell.
- **Midori**: I’m worried about the cost. Two hundred thousand is a lot. But thirty-four thousand a year is a leak. We need to stop the leak.
- **Shunichi**: I’m worried about the relationships. If we don’t fix the gate-house burden, we’re done. I need a plan that shares the pain.
- **May**: I’m worried about the time. If the truck comes early, the oversight is impossible. I need a plan that respects our sleep.
- **Asuka**: I’m worried about the fairness. The frontages are unequal. I need a plan that accounts for that.
## Round 3
