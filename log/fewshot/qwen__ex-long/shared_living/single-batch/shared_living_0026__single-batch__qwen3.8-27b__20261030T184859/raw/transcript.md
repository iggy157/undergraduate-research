## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Mio**: @Toshio, the ledger shows seventy percent of costs are fixed, like hall hire. But the bank statements show a third of renewals needed chasing. That’s a lot of work.
- **Jonathan**: @Mio, that chasing is exactly the pressure I’m worried about. If people feel hunted for money, they stop coming.
- **Takumi**: @Mio, fixed costs mean everyone pays for empty rooms. But the real issue is who does the chasing. I’ll show you the rota.
- **Toshio**: @Mio, the hours confirm it. Chasing individual payments took six hours a month. A yearly collection took one. The burden is real.
- **Kenji**: @Toshio, six hours is huge. If the system is too hard, people won’t take the job. We need something simple.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Mio**: @Toshio, so a flat fee is easier to manage? But if costs go up, we’re stuck. I need to know if we can afford it.
- **Jonathan**: @Mio, a flat fee might feel like a tax. If people don’t see the value, they hesitate. I saw that in the turnout.
- **Takumi**: @Jonathan, hesitation is one thing. But the sign-in sheets show a quarter of people do two-thirds of the work. That’s unfair.
- **Toshio**: @Takumi, the work is uneven, but the collection is the bottleneck. If we charge per event, the hours skyrocket.
- **Kenji**: @Takumi, I get it. But if we make it too complicated, new people won’t join. The taster forms say they don’t want a whole year at once.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Mio**: @Kenji, that’s a good point. If they don’t want a whole year, maybe a base fee plus event charges is better?
- **Jonathan**: @Mio, event charges made weekday turnout drop by a third. People stopped feeling like they belonged. I don’t want that.
- **Takumi**: @Jonathan, but if we don’t charge for events, the hard workers are subsidizing the ghosts. I want fairness.
- **Toshio**: @Takumi, fairness is good, but if the treasurer spends six hours a month chasing money, the club fails. I need a sustainable system.
- **Kenji**: @Toshio, I agree. But if we’re too strict, we lose the newcomers. The minute books show the old grades caused friction.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Mio**: I’m leaning towards a flat fee. It’s the easiest to manage. But I’m worried about the fixed costs.
- **Jonathan**: I’m worried about the flat fee feeling like a burden. I want to protect the quiet members.
- **Takumi**: I’m voting for whatever spreads the work. But I need to see the options first.
- **Toshio**: I need a system that minimizes the treasurer’s hours. I’m leaning towards the simplest option.
- **Kenji**: I want something welcoming. I’m hoping the options give us a choice.
