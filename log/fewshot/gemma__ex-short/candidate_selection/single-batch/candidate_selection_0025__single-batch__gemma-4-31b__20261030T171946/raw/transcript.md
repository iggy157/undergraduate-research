## Round 3
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Shizue**: The candidates are A: Mobile Branch, B: Maker Workshop, C: Language Circles, and D: Digital Archive. I worry A is too weather-dependent.
- **Jonathan**: A addresses the bus issue, but D addresses the late-night catalogue use. I lean toward A for the outer wards.
- **Rin**: I don't like D because the old digitisation project failed. C sounds like those evening groups that are still working!

- **Benjamin**: D is the most logically sound, as it avoids vehicle costs and staffing gaps.
