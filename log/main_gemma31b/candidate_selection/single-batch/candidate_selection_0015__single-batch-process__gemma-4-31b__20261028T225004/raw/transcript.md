### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Ryuji**: Suppliers are unreliable for some tech, and winter roads are a real problem. We can't rely on things we can't control.

- **George**: The grants are temporary. They cover a year, then hit the operating budget. Also, vehicle costs overran significantly last year.
- **May**: History repeats. A digitisation project failed years ago, but partner-run evening groups are still going strong without our help.

- **Ryuji**: @George, if vehicle costs are overrunning, a mobile service is a financial and logistical risk, especially with the road closures I found.
- **Yumi**: @Ryuji, but the people in those wards are stranded! We can't just ignore them because of a few snow days.
- **George**: @Yumi, I agree with the need, but the funding structure is a trap. We need something that doesn't create a budget cliff.
- **May**: @George, that's why we should look at the partner-run model. It's sustainable because the community carries it.
- **Daisuke**: @May, but who supervises them? My research shows we lack the internal training to manage outside facilitators safely.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Ryuji**: @Daisuke, that makes a Maker Workshop dangerous if the certification is gone. We need a low-dependency model.
- **Yumi**: @Daisuke, would Language Circles need the same technical supervision as power tools? Maybe that's a safer way to reach people.
- **George**: @Yumi, as long as the grant doesn't leave us stranded in year two. We need to use the endowment for capital.
- **May**: @George, the evening groups proved that community-led models work. We should avoid the "big project" failure of the archives.
- **Daisuke**: I just don't want the staff to burn out. If it's too complex to manage, it will fail.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Ryuji**: It seems we must balance accessibility with reliability. A fixed building is safe, but a mobile one is risky.
- **Yumi**: But a fixed building is useless to someone with no bus.
- **George**: We need a solution that fits the endowment and doesn't bleed the operating budget.
- **May**: Let's look at the candidates with these constraints in mind.
- **Daisuke**: I agree. Let's see what the options are.
