## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Shizue**: It seems our grants are quite short-term, and the roads north of the river can be quite unreliable in winter. We must be careful.
- **Jonathan**: The outer wards have lost their bus links, making it very hard for them to reach us. Also, people use the catalogue late at night.
- **Rin**: Guess what! People loved the old outreach stops, but the digitisation project from six years ago was a total mess!
- **Benjamin**: The grant structure is indeed precarious, and last year's vehicle costs were significantly over budget. We should avoid high-maintenance transport.
- **Minako**: We have skilled volunteers for repairs, but we lack staff trained to supervise outside facilitators. We cannot simply add more management.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Shizue**: @Benjamin, if the vehicles are so expensive, perhaps a mobile service is too risky for our budget?
- **Jonathan**: @Shizue, but the people in the outer wards are stranded. They cannot come to us.
- **Rin**: But the outreach stops were popular! Maybe we just need to do it better this time?
- **Benjamin**: @Rin, popularity does not solve the budget overrun. We need a service that doesn't bleed funds by the second year.
- **Minako**: @Jonathan, if we move services, we still need people to run them. We have no one to supervise external partners.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Shizue**: @Minako, what about the volunteers? Could they help with something like a workshop?
- **Jonathan**: A workshop stays in one place. It doesn't help the people who have no bus.
- **Rin**: What about the language groups? They sounded like the ones that lasted six years ago!
- **Benjamin**: @Rin, that is an interesting observation. If they are self-sustaining, they fit our financial constraints.
- **Minako**: Only if they don't require professional supervision from our already stretched staff.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Shizue**: I worry about any plan that relies on the roads or short-term grants.
- **Jonathan**: I still feel the lack of transport is the most urgent gap to fill.
- **Rin**: I just want something that actually works and doesn't get forgotten!
- **Benjamin**: We need a balance of accessibility and fiscal sustainability.
- **Minako**: I will support the option that requires the least amount of new supervisory overhead.
## Round 3
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Shizue**: The candidates are a Mobile Branch, a Maker Workshop, Language Circles, or a Digital Archive. I fear the Mobile Branch due to the roads.
- **Jonathan**: The Mobile Branch is the only one that solves the bus link crisis. The others keep the service centralized.
- **Rin**: The Digital Archive is like the old project that failed! Let's do the Language Circles or the Mobile Branch!
- **Benjamin**: The Mobile Branch is a financial trap given the running costs. The Language Circles seem the most stable.
- **Minako**: The Maker Workshop is risky because our tool certification expert retired. Language Circles are the safest for staff.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Shizue**: @Benjamin, I agree. The Language Circles seem gentle and sustainable.
- **Jonathan**: @Minako, is there no way to supervise a mobile service?

- **Benjamin**: @Jonathan, the cost is the issue, not just the supervision. The budget would thin out too quickly.
- **Minako**: I cannot support the Workshop or the Archive. The Circles are the most viable.

- **Minako**: I agree. It is the most manageable.
