## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Yumi**: It turns out people in the outer wards really loved the outreach! But some old programs just stopped.
- **Benjamin**: It seems some digital efforts from years ago were left unfinished, while community-led groups survived.
- **Shion**: We have retired tradespeople, but no one on staff is actually trained to supervise outside facilitators.
- **Minako**: The grants only cover the first year. After that, the operating budget has to pick up the slack.
- **Daisuke**: The bus links are gone in two wards. People have to travel an hour now.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Yumi**: @Daisuke, that's terrible! We have to do something to reach them if they can't get here.
- **Benjamin**: @Shion, your point about supervision is critical. We cannot implement a technical service without oversight.
- **Shion**: Exactly. And the person who certified the power tools is gone. A workshop would be a liability.
- **Minako**: Also, the vehicle lines overran last year. A mobile service might be expensive to maintain.
- **Daisuke**: But the road closures didn't stop the community halls... maybe we can use those?
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Yumi**: If the community groups survived on their own, maybe we should support something like that?
- **Benjamin**: The data shows remote catalogue use peaks late at night. Our physical hours are a bottleneck.
- **Shion**: A digital portal wouldn't require physical supervision or tool certification. It's safer.
- **Minako**: But the grant terms mean we need a plan for year two. A portal has lower recurring costs than a van.
- **Daisuke**: I still feel the mobile service is the only way to help those without buses.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Yumi**: I agree with Daisuke, but we have to make sure it doesn't just disappear after a year.
- **Benjamin**: Logic suggests the Digital Archive is the most sustainable, given the past failures of digitisation.
- **Shion**: I agree. It avoids the staffing gap and the liability of the workshop.
- **Minako**: I am leaning toward the portal or the language circles, provided the funding is stable.
- **Daisuke**: I... I just don't want the outer wards to be forgotten again.
