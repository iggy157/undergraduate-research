## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Asuka**: The neighboring block had residents stop attending meetings due to payment disputes. We must avoid creating financial enemies.
- **Yumi**: I found that the same flats have held the covered spots for years without any review. That explains the grumbling.
- **Ryuji**: It's worse than we thought. People are shifting other bikes just to leave, and some spots are completely exposed to rain.
- **Shunichi**: Our ledger is nearly empty; any upgrades require a levy. Meanwhile, the ward park is expensive and has a long waitlist.
- **Toshio**: The current cash collection is already a burden. Adding per-space billing would increase the treasurer's workload significantly.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Asuka**: @Yumi, if the arrangement has been static for five years, a sudden change might be jarring. We need a smooth transition.
- **Yumi**: But the daily riders told me they'd actually prefer to pay for a guaranteed spot than have an equal claim.
- **Ryuji**: If we keep fixed spots, the "rainy" spots will always be the source of conflict. The physical layout is the problem.
- **Shunichi**: If residents are willing to pay, we could solve our budget issues. It's the most rational way to allocate scarce resources.
- **Toshio**: @Shunichi, but who manages the money? The handover file shows the current process is already tedious.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Asuka**: @Toshio, is the physical effort of a new rack system any better?
- **Yumi**: I just want everyone to feel it's fair. A lottery might be the kindest way?
- **Ryuji**: A lottery doesn't fix the "shoving match" in the morning or the rain. We need a structural change.
- **Shunichi**: A lottery is a gamble. Paid allocation ensures those who value the space most get it, and the building profits.
- **Toshio**: The display frame trial showed that double-tier racks are physically demanding and require regular maintenance.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Asuka**: So we have a choice between administrative burden, social friction, or physical difficulty.
- **Yumi**: I'm worried that paying for spots might make some neighbors feel excluded.
- **Ryuji**: I'm more worried about the bikes leaning on each other and the morning chaos.
- **Shunichi**: Let's be realistic. We can't afford new equipment without a levy, and we can't ignore the revenue potential.
- **Toshio**: I prefer the option with the least amount of monthly paperwork and maintenance.

## Round 3
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Asuka**: Option A creates financial tension, B is a temporary fix, and C creates physical chaos. I lean toward B for stability.
- **Yumi**: I like B too. A lottery is fair and doesn't make people feel like they are "buying" a right.
- **Ryuji**: I strongly oppose B. It doesn't solve the congestion. Option C, shared racks, removes the "my spot" mentality entirely.
- **Shunichi**: Option C is a nightmare to maintain and expensive to install. Option A is the only one that makes financial sense.
- **Toshio**: Option C requires greasing and physical effort. Option A is a paperwork nightmare. Option B is the lowest maintenance.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Asuka**: @Ryuji, wouldn't shared racks just lead to more "leaning" and complaints since no one is responsible for a spot?
- **Yumi**: Exactly! And if the racks are hard to use, people will just park wherever they want.
- **Ryuji**: At least it's honest. Right now, the "covered" spots are a hidden privilege.
- **Shunichi**: @Toshio, surely the benefit of a building fund outweighs a few hours of bookkeeping?
- **Toshio**: Not if the treasurer quits. We've seen roles lapse before. I cannot support Option A.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Asuka**: If we go with B, we reset the unfairness Yumi found without the risks of A or C.
- **Yumi**: I agree. It's a fresh start for everyone every year.
- **Ryuji**: It's a lazy solution. We are ignoring the physical congestion and the rain.
- **Shunichi**: It's not lazy, it's just inefficient. We are leaving money on the table and ignoring the market value of the spaces.
- **Toshio**: Efficiency is useless if the system is unsustainable. B is the only one that doesn't create a new job.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Asuka**: It seems B is the most logical compromise for social harmony.
- **Yumi**: I'm voting for B!
- **Ryuji**: I'll stick with C, but it seems I'm outnumbered.
- **Shunichi**: I'll vote A. I cannot support a system that ignores the financial reality.
- **Toshio**: My vote is for B.
