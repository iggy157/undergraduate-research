## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Misaki**: The neighboring block had people stop attending meetings over fees. That's a warning.
- **Shizue**: It's quite a mess. People are shifting other bikes just to leave in the morning.

- **George**: Those double-tier racks are a nightmare to maintain and hard to use.
- **Mio**: It's true! The same flats have held the best spots for five years without review.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Misaki**: @Mio, that explains the resentment, but we must avoid a system that breeds more.
- **Shizue**: And the rain! The bikes past the downpipe are getting soaked. It's not fair.

- **May**: If we don't charge, we can't fix the rain issue or the layout.
- **George**: I can't support anything that adds hours of monthly paperwork to the association.
- **Mio**: A lottery would be the fairest way to break the five-year streak of the same people.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Misaki**: A lottery is neutral, but it doesn't solve the physical crowding.
- **Shizue**: I just want everyone to be able to leave without a struggle.
- **May**: Money solves the budget and the demand. It's the most professional way.
- **George**: As long as the administration is minimal, I can be flexible.
- **Mio**: Let's see the options and decide based on these facts.
