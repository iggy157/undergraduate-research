## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Misaki**: It seems the same people have held the best spots for years without any review. Also, many complaints about bikes leaning.
- **Shizue**: I saw that people are shifting other bikes to get out, and some spots get quite wet in the rain.
- **May**: The building account is nearly empty for capital work, and the station park is expensive with a long waitlist.
- **George**: New racks require maintenance and effort. Also, the treasurer already spends hours on small cash collections.
- **Mio**: Daily riders are actually willing to pay for a spot, but the neighbors had issues with people skipping meetings over fees.
#### Turn 2 of 4

- **Mio**: But the current system is unfair. The daily riders feel the imbalance.
#### Turn 3 of 4

- **Shizue**: I agree. We shouldn't let a few bike spots turn us against each other.
- **May**: If we can't afford new equipment, we have to work with what we have.
- **George**: A simple system is a sustainable system.
- **Mio**: @Misaki, do you think a lottery would be better than paying?
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Misaki**: A lottery is fair, but it changes every year.

- **May**: As long as it doesn't cost the building money we don't have.
- **George**: A lottery is low maintenance compared to monthly billing.
- **Mio**: I think the daily riders would prefer something more stable, but fairness comes first.
