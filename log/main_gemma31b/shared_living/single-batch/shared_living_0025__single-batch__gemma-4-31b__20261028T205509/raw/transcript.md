- **Rin**: @Misaki, if people are already arguing, making the math more complex with meters or templates will just make it worse.
- **Shunichi**: @Ryuji, exactly. We can't ignore that some of us are paying for others' desktop computers and heaters.
- **Misaki**: @Rin, I agree. We need a solution that is simple enough that it doesn't require a "manager" who everyone ends up hating.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Mio**: What if we just agreed on a set amount for the season? That way we don't have to think about it every time we flip a switch.
- **Ryuji**: A seasonal fixed rate would stabilize the monthly cost, but we'd still have to settle the difference at the end of the year.
- **Rin**: As long as that year-end settle isn't a huge project, it sounds better than doing it every single month.
- **Shunichi**: It doesn't solve the unfairness of room usage, but it's better than the current chaos.
- **Misaki**: It removes the monthly friction. That's the most important thing for the atmosphere of the house.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Mio**: I really think the "paying ahead" vibe is what we need to feel at home here.
- **Ryuji**: I can accept a fixed rate if it prevents the "second rent" feeling in winter.
- **Rin**: I'm on board if it means less time spent on spreadsheets.
- **Shunichi**: I'll concede on the usage imbalance if it means we stop the bickering.
- **Misaki**: I agree. Let's go for the most peaceful option.
