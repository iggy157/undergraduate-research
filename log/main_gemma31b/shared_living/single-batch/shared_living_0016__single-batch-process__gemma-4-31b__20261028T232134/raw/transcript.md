#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Minato**: The treasurer spends six hours a month chasing session money. Mid-year joiners also need a lot of manual calculations.

- **Minako**: Past named grades caused friction and discouraged lower tiers. Also, many non-joiners refuse to commit to a full year upfront.
- **May**: Turnout dropped after the summer door charges. Some quiet members also feel uneasy about the cost per visit.

- **May**: Exactly. People stop feeling like the club is "theirs" when they have to pay every time they walk in.
- **Shunichi**: While that's true, it's unfair that the regulars carry the weight while others pay the same for nothing.

- **May**: We need something that feels welcoming but doesn't make people hesitate at the door.
- **Shunichi**: Efficiency is fine, but we need to address the imbalance of use and effort.
