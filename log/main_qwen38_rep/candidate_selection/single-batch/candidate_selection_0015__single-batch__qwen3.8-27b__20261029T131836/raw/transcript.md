#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Ryuji**: @George, the equipment budget was under last year. Could that cover the initial vehicle setup?

- **Yumi**: I support D. It solves the access problem directly.
- **George**: I am concerned about the long-term funding. But D has the strongest demand evidence.
