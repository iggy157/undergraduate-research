## Round 2
### Talk
#### Turn 1 of 4
- **Asuka**: The taster forms are clear. Nine out of fourteen said they wouldn't commit to a whole year at once.
- **Benjamin**: The treasurer's logs are brutal. Chasing individual payments took six hours a month. A yearly collection took one.
- **Jonathan**: The sign-in sheets show a quarter of members do two-thirds of the work. Eighteen people never signed in.
- **Shunichi**: The winter social feedback confirms it. Quiet members feel uneasy about the cost per visit.
- **Takumi**: The ledger shows seventy percent of costs are fixed. Hall hire and insurance don't care if anyone comes.
#### Turn 2 of 4
- **Asuka**: @Benjamin, if we charge per event, the admin load will explode.
- **Benjamin**: @Asuka, yes. That is why I am against activity-based charging. It is unsustainable for the treasurer.
- **Jonathan**: @Takumi, if costs are fixed, a flat fee makes sense. But what about the eighteen who never come?
- **Shunichi**: @Jonathan, they pay for the privilege of belonging, even if they don't use it.
- **Takumi**: @Shunichi, but the bank statements show a third of renewals needed reminders. People struggle with big upfront sums.
#### Turn 3 of 4
- **Asuka**: We need a middle ground. A base fee covers the fixed costs, but we need to be careful with extras.
- **Benjamin**: A tiered system might work. A low base fee for everyone, and higher tiers for those who use more.
- **Jonathan**: But the minute books from the named-grade years show friction. People stopped putting themselves forward.
- **Shunichi**: @Jonathan, that is a risk. But the current system is failing the quiet members.
- **Takumi**: I agree with Benjamin. We need to reduce the admin burden. A flat fee is simplest, but maybe too rigid.
#### Turn 4 of 4
- **Asuka**: Let's look at the options. We have to balance fairness, admin load, and accessibility.
- **Benjamin**: Option B, Tiered Membership, seems the most logical. It allows choice without excessive admin.
- **Jonathan**: I am worried about the social friction, but the data supports a tiered approach.
- **Shunichi**: I can live with it if the lower tier is genuinely accessible.
- **Takumi**: I will support it if it simplifies the collection process.
